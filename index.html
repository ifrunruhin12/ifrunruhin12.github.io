Projects

Shrutik

Shrutik (শ্রুতিক) began as an attempt to make voice technology more inclusive for underrepresented languages. Built under my organization, Onuronon, the platform allows communities to contribute voice data for open and accessible AI systems. I initiated the project and currently lead its architecture and engineering.

BGCE-CMS

It started as an archive for “Best Golang Community Ever”, a Bangladeshi community of Go developers that I help manage. Initially, the project was meant to be a simple open-source vault where members could share articles, notes, tools, and learning resources they created themselves. Over time, it evolved into something much larger: a collaborative knowledge hub built by the community, for the community. I’ve been one of the pioneers of the project and remain one of its most active contributors.
